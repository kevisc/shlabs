The two apps

Cadence →

A four-channel performance mixer and master clock.

Separate stems on your machine. Let Assist hold the mix while you play. Send MIDI clock to your hardware.

Standalone app · macOS and Windows, in beta

Phosphor →

An audio-reactive video synth.

It runs on the GPU and reads the audio in real time. Lock it to Cadence or to your DAW. Send it fullscreen to a projector.

VST3 · AU · Standalone
