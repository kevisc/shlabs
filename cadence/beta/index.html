Apple Silicon Macs need macOS 14 or newer. Intel Macs are not supported in this beta. Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" on first run: choose More info, then Run anyway. Anyone hosting Cell in Cadence needs the Cell above: the looping-note fix was a fix on both sides, so take this build of it alongside Cadence.

The full seven-plugin bundle is not hosted here. At 86 MB it is larger than the rest of this site put together, and it carries no change since the alpha. Ask us and we will send you a link.

Bugs, impressions and questions to shlabs.contact@gmail.com. Most useful: what you did, what you expected, what happened, and the log (CADENCE wordmark, About, Show log file).
