Apple Silicon Macs need macOS 14 or newer. Intel Macs are not supported in this beta. Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" on first run: choose More info, then Run anyway. The seven SHLabs plugins are listed above one by one, so you can take only the ones you host in Cadence. Each zip holds the AU and the VST3 and its own install card. Cell, Contour, Glue, Spazio, Stesso and Tonnetz are universal builds; Phosphor 0.3.0 is Apple Silicon only. All are ad-hoc signed like Cadence, so the first launch asks once.

Bugs, impressions and questions to shlabs.contact@gmail.com. Most useful: what you did, what you expected, what happened, and the log (CADENCE wordmark, About, Show log file).
