01 Flagships

Paid · Coming soon
Performance & live · Standalone

Cadence

A four-channel performance mixer and master clock.

A standalone app with beatgrid clip players and quantized launch. It detects track structure, separates stems on your machine and can kill them on the bar. Assist can carry a transition. A Composer timeline lays out a set in advance. MIDI or audio that you play records back onto its channel as a loop. A sample-accurate MIDI clock drives your modular, drum machines and grooveboxes.

4-channel mixer 24-PPQN MIDI clock OSC broadcast macOS · Windows beta in testing
Visuals · VST3 · AU · Standalone

Kinora

An audio-reactive video synth.

A GPU engine with thirty-two scenes and a particle system of 100,000 points. Band envelopes and onset detectors drive the image. It locks to Cadence or to your host playhead and runs fullscreen on a projector.

GLSL engine 138 presets Syphon out MIDI learn macOS · Windows in CI
The bundle

Cadence sends its clock, beat phase, band energy, kick onsets and drop intensity to Kinora over OSC, so the visuals follow the set. The bundle also includes Cell, Schlagzeugs, Contour, Spazio, Lacuna, Legato, Stesso and Tonnetz.

04 The studio

How we work
State

Full recall

Generative modules are seedable. Every plugin recalls its full state. A saved patch plays the same on every machine.

Interface

Controls first

Panels and plugin windows carry controls, not decoration. Drag-to-assign modulation, MIDI learn and sensible defaults. No telemetry.

Engine

Named methods

The tools use analog-modelled circuits, feedback, sampling and stochastic processes. Product pages name the method behind a tool where one applies.

The studio

An independent studio in Switzerland. One person builds everything here: plugins and standalone apps for the stage and the DAW, and free modules for VCV Rack 2.
