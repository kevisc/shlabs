Apple Silicon only, macOS 11 or newer. Standalone, VST3 and AU. Windows follows.

Install

  1. Download the zip and unzip it.
  2. Copy Phosphor.app to /Applications.
  3. Copy Phosphor.vst3 to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and Phosphor.component to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components.
  4. Right-click each of the three, choose Open, and confirm.
  5. Rescan plugins in your host if it does not list Phosphor.

The build is signed ad hoc, not by Apple, so a plain double click is refused and you have to right-click and choose Open once per item.

Known limitations

  • Signed ad hoc, not notarized. Notarization comes with the release.
  • Apple Silicon only, macOS 11 or newer.
  • A recording splits into two files if you open or close fullscreen mid-take.
  • The standalone hears nothing until you enable an input, or until Cadence feeds it over OSC.
  • No Windows zip in this round. Windows follows.

The licence is EULA.txt inside the zip. NOTICES.txt lists the third-party code.
