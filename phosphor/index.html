Phosphor

A video synthesiser that listens.

A GPU-native GLSL engine with five scene families and a hundred-thousand-particle system, driven by real band envelopes and onset detection, locked to your clock and thrown fullscreen onto the projector. Insert it on a track and the audio passes through untouched.

Premium · VST3 · AU · StandalonemacOS

What it does

01 Scene engine

Five families of image

Everything renders on the GPU in GLSL. Morphable pattern Fields cover more than twelve forms; Tunnel is a flythrough; Fractals run Julia, Mandelbrot and Burning Ship; Spectrum draws bars, radial and spectrograph views from a true FFT; Ambient holds nebula, aurora, caustics and lava.

02 Particles

A hundred thousand of them

A GPGPU particle system running upward of 100,000 points with trails and reflections, simulated entirely on the graphics card so the count buys you density rather than dropped frames.

03 Reactivity

It hears more than level

Separate envelopes for overall level and for bass, mid and high, plus onset detectors for kick, snare and hat. Spectral centroid tilts the colour toward the brightness of the sound. One master REACT control sets how hard all of it pushes.

04 Sync

Three ways to stay in time

Lock to Cadence over OSC and follow beat, phase, BPM, transport, band energy, onsets and drop intensity. Or take the host playhead inside a DAW. Or run standalone on Ableton Link. Motion stays on the grid either way.

05 Performance

Built for the room

Fullscreen to a second display or projector, Evolve for hands-free generative drift, Auto for preset roaming and Flow to morph between them — cut, smooth or slow. More than 36 presets and 15 colour palettes, layered dual-engine blending, a video-clip layer with reactive blending, and shockwave ripples on the kick.

06 I/O & control

Out to the rest of the rig

Syphon output on macOS feeds your VJ software or media server; H.264 recording and PNG snapshots capture the set. MIDI-learn covers every control, and a phone remote joins over the local network by QR code.

VST3 · AU · Standalone. macOS now; a Windows build is in CI with the visuals and reactivity intact — Syphon and recording stay macOS-only.
