Kinora

An audio-reactive video synthesiser. Until November 2026 it was called Phosphor. The Kinora was a home moving-picture viewer, made from 1896 to 1914.

A GLSL engine on the GPU, with thirty-two scenes and a particle system of 100,000 points. Band envelopes and onset detection drive the image. It locks to your clock or to the beat it finds in the audio. Output goes fullscreen to a projector or floats over the music you play. When inserted on a track, it passes the audio through unchanged.

What it does

01 Scene engine

Thirty-two scenes

Everything renders on the GPU in GLSL. Fields holds more than twelve morphable patterns. Fractal runs Julia, Mandelbrot and Burning Ship. Spectrum and Waveform draw the audio itself from an FFT and an oscilloscope. Other scenes include a tunnel flythrough, a retro horizon, flowers, jellyfish, 3D pipes, chrome, mazes and datamosh. Press G to open the scene gallery.

02 Particles

100,000 points

A GPGPU particle system with more than 100,000 points, trails and reflections. The simulation runs entirely on the graphics card.

03 Reactivity

Bands and onsets

Separate envelopes for overall level and for bass, mid and high. Onset detectors for kick, snare and hat. Spectral centroid tilts the colour with the brightness of the sound. One master REACT control sets the overall response.

04 Sync

Clock sources

Lock to Cadence over OSC and follow beat, phase, BPM, transport, band energy, onsets and drop intensity. Use the host playhead inside a DAW. Or let Kinora find the beat in the audio, for a stream or a record with no clock.

05 Just listen

A visualiser for the music you play

On a Mac, Kinora captures what any app plays, such as Spotify, Apple Music or a browser, with no cable and no loopback driver. A hands-free director runs over it. Kinora asks the player what is playing, so a new track changes the scene and the album art sets the palette. You can float it over the player, with click-through if you want, or set it as a live desktop wallpaper with a menu bar icon. A flash limiter keeps the picture under three flashes a second. The controls fade when the mouse rests.

06 Performance

For live use

Fullscreen output to a second display or projector. Evolve gives hands-free generative drift. Auto moves between presets, and Flow morphs between them: cut, smooth or slow. 138 factory presets and 15 colour palettes, layered dual-engine blending, a video-clip layer with reactive blending, and shockwave ripples on the kick.

07 I/O & control

Outputs and remote control

Syphon output on macOS feeds VJ software or a media server. H.264 recording and PNG snapshots capture the output. MIDI-learn covers every control, and a phone remote joins over the local network by QR code.

VST3 · AU · Standalone. macOS now. A Windows build is in CI with the visuals and reactivity. Syphon and recording stay macOS-only.

The bundle

Bundle pricing at release

Kinora locks to Cadence

Cadence broadcasts clock phase, beat, BPM, transport, master band energy, kick onsets and a drop-aware intensity signal over OSC. Kinora uses them to put motion on the beat, move parameters with the bands, react to the kick and raise the image into the drop. The bundle also includes the plugin line: Cell, Schlagzeugs, Contour, Spazio, Lacuna, Legato, Stesso and Tonnetz.
